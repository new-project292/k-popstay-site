About This Project

When BTS announced their 2026 Busan comeback concerts, accommodation prices in Busan surged to extraordinary levels — with some listings reaching 10x normal rates. This kind of price gouging leaves ordinary fans with no choice but to pay or give up on attending.

K-POPSTAY BUSAN is our answer: a civic initiative connecting ARMY fans with verified Busan citizen hosts who open their homes fairly, without exploiting the moment. Every stay is free of charge for guests, with a full deposit refund system to ensure trust on both sides.

This is not charity — it is community. Busan citizens stepping forward to say: "You are welcome here."

K-POPSTAY BUSAN

What is K-POPSTAY BUSAN?

01

Verified, Trustworthy Hosts

Every host is a verified Busan citizen vetted through Wehome's platform. Guests can book with confidence knowing their host is genuine, accountable, and community-spirited.

02

100% Refundable Security Deposit

Selected ARMY guests are required to pay a security deposit of KRW 50,000 per person. Upon check-in, the full deposit amount will be refunded in the form of a Busan Tourism Gift Card. The Busan Tourism Gift Card can be used like cash at participating businesses throughout Busan.

Stay Costs & Deposit Policy

Guest Cost: KRW 0

Your stay is completely free of charge. K-POPSTAY BUSAN is a civic initiative — Busan citizen hosts voluntarily open their homes to global ARMY at no cost.
