Why Busan

More Than a Concert

Busan is the hometown of Jimin and Jungkook. The city is planning large-scale entertainment tied to the tour, making it a festival-style destination beyond just the stadium.

✨

BTS THE CITY ARIRANG Busan

Citywide programming including a welcome center, stamp rally, landmark lighting, hotel packages, and a drone light show at Gwangandaegyo Bridge — June 12 & 13 at 10:00 PM KST.

Getting There

How to Get to Busan

Three main options depending on where you're coming from.

OptionFromDurationNotes
🚄 KTX Seoul Station ~2.5–3 hrs Most popular option. Book in advance — seats sell out fast during concert weekend.
✈️ Fly Other Asian cities Varies Arrive at Gimhae International Airport. Good for international travelers coming directly to Busan.
🚌 Express Bus Seoul & other cities 4–5+ hrs Cheapest option but significantly longer. Not recommended for concert weekend given time constraints.
Recommended plan: Arrive via Seoul → 1–3 days sightseeing → take KTX to Busan → stay for concert weekend → return to Seoul after. Avoid trying to return to Seoul immediately after the show — late finish + heavy crowds make this impractical.
Concert Day Transport

Getting to the Stadium

Busan Asiad Main Stadium is in Yeonje-gu. Nearest subway stations are Sports Complex Station and Sajik Station.

🚇
Take the Subway
Subway is strongly recommended over taxis on concert day. Traffic near the venue may be restricted. Plan for longer travel time than usual.
🚌
Shuttle Buses
Round-trip shuttle buses available via platforms like Klook. Pickup from Busan Station, Seomyeon, and Haeundae. Book in advance.
Preparation

Concert Day Tips

A 7:00 PM show means crowds build from early afternoon. Be prepared.

Arrive several hours before 7:00 PM

Safety

Solo Traveler Tips

Busan is a safe city, but a little preparation goes a long way.

📍
Stay Connected
Share your hotel location with a trusted contact back home. Save your hotel address in Korean on your phone — it makes taxi rides and navigation much easier.
🚕
Getting Around Safely
Use official taxis or reliable ride apps. Avoid unofficial ticket and merchandise deals. Know the last subway departure time so you're not stranded.
🛡️
Travel Insurance
Consider travel insurance that covers flights, hotel, and medical expenses. Concert weekends can be unpredictable — it's worth the peace of mind.
🎟️
Avoid Ticket Scams
Only buy tickets through NOL Ticket (official seller). Scams targeting international fans are common. Verify all purchases via official BTS/Weverse channels.
