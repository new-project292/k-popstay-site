    BTS Busan

    Holy Sites & Pilgrimage Route

    Busan is where it all began. Here's a suggested one-day route, organised so you're not doubling back across the city. Use Naver Map (네이버 지도) — Google Maps doesn't navigate well in Korea.

    🏖️
    Morning · All Members · Line 2
    Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장)
    Korea's most famous beach, appears throughout early BTS content. Walk the full length before the crowds — morning light here is genuinely beautiful.
    Haeundae Station (해운대역) Exit 3 or 5
    🎨
    RM Connection · Haeundae-gu
    Busan Museum of Art (부산시립미술관) & F1963
    RM is documented to visit art spaces in Busan. F1963 — a repurposed wire factory turned cultural complex — has bookshops, galleries, and café courtyards.
    58 APEC-ro, Haeundae-gu · Short taxi from beach
    🌉
    Afternoon · All Members · Line 2
    Gwangalli & Borahae Street (광안리 보라해 거리)
    Unofficial spiritual home of Busan ARMY. Purple-themed cafés and murals line the beachfront strip. Come in the evening when Gwangan Bridge lights up behind you.
    Gwangan Station (광안역) Exit 3 or 5 · 10 min walk
    🌿
    V (태형) · Late Afternoon · Busanjin-gu
    Busan Citizens Park (부산시민공원) — V's Walk
    V has been photographed walking through this park. A lovely urban park with a stream, forested sections, and a cultural museum inside. Late afternoon light is extraordinary.
    Bujeon Station (부전역) Line 1, Exit 7 · 15 min walk
    🏙️
    Jimin (지민) + Jungkook (정국) · Lines 1 & 2
    Seomyeon (서면) — Their Busan
    Where both members spent their school years. The underground mall (서면 지하상가) is massive — K-beauty, fashion, and late-night food at very local prices.
    Seomyeon Station (서면역) Lines 1 & 2
    🏫
    Jimin (지민) School · Line 3 Orange
    Busan Arts High School Vicinity (부산예술고등학교 인근)
    Jimin's school in Buk-gu. The school is not open to visitors, but the quiet residential streets around it offer a more personal pilgrimage.
    Deokcheon Station (덕천역) Line 3, Exit 2 · Be respectful of residents
    🎨
    Bonus · Any Day · Saha-gu
    Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을)
    One of the most photographed places in Korea — a hillside village of colourful stacked houses with murals at every turn. Appears in content from multiple members. Go in the morning to beat tour groups.
    Toseong Station (토성역) Line 1 · then Maul Bus 1-1 or 2
    Eat Like a Local

    Busan Food You Have to Try

    Busan has its own food culture distinct from Seoul. These are the dishes that define the city — not tourist food.

    🍲
    Busan's Soul Food
    Dwaeji Gukbap
    Pork and rice soup served in a huge bowl with kimchi and green onions. Busan's unofficial city dish. Eaten any time of day, including 2am after a concert.
    돼지국밥 · dwaej-i guk-bap
    🐟
    Jagalchi Market · Nampo-dong
    Raw Seafood (Hoe)
    Korea's largest seafood market is in Busan. Buy fresh-caught fish from the stalls downstairs and take it upstairs to eat right away. Overwhelming in the best way.
    회 · hoe · Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장)
    🍢
    Street Food · Nampo-dong
    Eomuk (Fish Cake)
    Flat skewered fish cake soaked in broth. A Busan street food icon — eaten standing at the stall with a cup of the broth on the side. About ₩500–1,000 per skewer.
    어묵 · eo-muk · around Nampo-dong (남포동)
    🍗
    Late Night
    Fried Chicken (Chimaek)
    Korean fried chicken with beer — one of the great combinations in world cuisine. Order half-and-half (반반): half plain fried, half spicy glazed. Delivered in 30 min or found at any neighbourhood chicken shop.
    치맥 · chi-maek · 반반 (half-half)
    🫕
    Comfort Food
    Milmyeon
    Busan's own cold wheat noodle dish — lighter and chewier than Seoul-style naengmyeon. A summer staple you can only truly get in Busan.
    밀면 · mil-myeon
    🧁
    Café Culture
    Bingsu & Korean Desserts
    Shaved ice with red bean, fruit, or condensed milk — essential in June heat. Busan's café scene along Gwangalli Beach has some of the best in the country.
    빙수 · bing-su · along Gwangalli (광안리)
    💡
    Ordering tip: Not spicy → say "안 맵게 해주세요" (an maep-ge hae-ju-se-yo). Works about 80% of the time. If the restaurant has no English menu, point at the menu and say "이거 하나 주세요" (i-geo ha-na ju-se-yo) — "one of this please." They will understand.
    Korea Know-How

    Survival Tips for Korea

    Tap any topic to expand. Stuff most guides don't tell you until you've already been confused for a day.

    Google Maps does not work properly in Korea. Due to national mapping restrictions it cannot give accurate walking or transit directions. The apps below take 2 minutes to set up and will save you hours of confusion.
    🟢
    Naver Map (네이버 지도) Install ↗
    Best for walking, transit & driving. Switch to English in settings. Make this your main nav app.
    App Store Google Play
    🟡
    Kakao Metro (카카오 지하철) Install ↗
    Live arrival times & last train countdowns. Check before leaving the venue post-show.
    App Store Google Play
    🚕
    Kakao T (카카오T) Install ↗
    Korea's Uber. Pay by card in-app. Book before leaving the venue on concert nights — don't wait until outside.
    App Store Google Play
    🚌
    T-Money Card (티머니)
    Buy at any CU, GS25, 7-Eleven. Load ₩30,000–50,000. Tap on/off every gate and bus. No cash needed for all transit.
    SituationWhat to Do
    Card paymentsVisa / Mastercard accepted almost everywhere. Korea is extremely card-friendly — restaurants, shops, convenience stores.
    Cash withdrawalAny GS25 or 7-Eleven ATM accepts international cards. Fee ~₩3,000. Works even if your card fails at bank ATMs.
    Markets & stallsJagalchi Market, pojangmacha — cash preferred. Bring ₩30,000–50,000 in ₩10,000 notes.
    SIM / mobile dataBuy eSIM or SIM at Gimhae Airport, convenience stores, or Homeplus/E-Mart. Don't rely on roaming — mobile data is essential.
    💡
    One of the genuine wonders of Korean life: almost any food delivered to a private residence in 20–30 minutes at restaurant prices. Apps are Baemin (배달의민족) and Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠) — both in Korean, but your host can help you order. A great option for concert nights when everywhere nearby is packed.
    As part of K-POPSTAY BUSAN, you will receive a Busan Tourism Gift Card when your deposit is refunded after your stay. Works like cash at thousands of local spots — restaurants, traditional markets, cafés, cultural sites. Look for the 부산관광 상품권 가맹점 sticker. Hand it to the cashier and say: "이만원 써주세요" = "Please use ₩20,000 of this." Does not work at large chains or online.
    Quick Korean

    Phrases That Will Make Locals Smile

    You don't need to memorise these. But if you try even one, you'll get a reaction you won't forget — especially in Busan.

    Thank You
    감사합니다
    gam-sa-ham-ni-da
    The formal version — always works
    Excuse Me / Sorry
    죄송합니다
    jwe-song-ham-ni-da
    Works when squeezing past people too
    How Much?
    얼마예요?
    eol-ma-ye-yo
    They'll show you on a calculator
    One of This, Please
    이거 하나 주세요
    i-geo ha-na ju-se-yo
    Point at the menu while saying this
    Not Spicy Please
    안 맵게 해주세요
    an maep-ge hae-ju-se-yo
    Works about 80% of the time
    Where's the Bathroom?
    화장실이 어디예요?
    hwa-jang-si-ri eo-di-ye-yo
    Universally understood
    Delicious!
    맛있어요!
    ma-si-sseo-yo
    Say this to the chef — they'll love it
    I Love BTS
    저는 BTS를 사랑해요
    jeo-neun BTS-reul sa-rang-hae-yo
    Instant conversation starter in Busan
